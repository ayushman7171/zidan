A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Simple answers.
No surprises.

Can I use the website without internet?

Visit once with an internet connection so the app and guides can be cached. Later offline access depends on your browser retaining that data. A first visit still needs internet.

Are the businesses and hubs real?

No. They are clearly labelled samples showing how a rural enterprise platform could work. They are not verified listings or places you can visit.

Where do support requests go?

They stay in your browser on this device. The demo backend validates the form but does not store it in a shared database or send it to a support team. You can download or delete your copies.

Is everything available in Marathi?

Yes! When you switch to Marathi mode, each and every text across all pages, guides, tools, and businesses is completely presented in Marathi.
